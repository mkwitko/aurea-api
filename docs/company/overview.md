# AUREA – Company Overview

## 1. Who We Are
- **Name:** AUREA (AI Growth Operating System)
- **Type:** B2B SaaS
- **Core Offering:** AI‑powered platform that orchestrates growth loops – from goal definition to learning and context updates.
- **Tagline / Value Proposition:** *"Automate growth intelligence, so teams focus on impact."*

## 2. Vision & Mission
- **Vision:** Empower every SaaS business to achieve sustainable, data‑driven growth without heavy manual processes.
- **Mission:** Provide an intelligent growth brain that centralises business context, generates measurable goals, designs strategies, runs experiments and continuously learns.

## 3. Products & Services
| Product / Service | Description |
|---|---|
| **Growth Brain Engine** | Central knowledge graph and reasoning layer that stores Business Context, Goals, Strategies, Experiments, etc. |
| **AI Agent Suite** | Agents that research, draft strategies, design experiments, and suggest actions. |
| **Integrations** | Connectors to CRMs, analytics, ad platforms, ticketing systems, etc. |
| **Implementation & Support** | On‑boarding, custom integration, and ongoing advisory services. |

## 4. Market & Positioning
- **Target Market:** Mid‑size B2B SaaS companies (revenues $5M‑$50M) that need to scale customer acquisition and retention.
- **Positioning Statement:** *The specialized AI growth assistant for SaaS businesses that want measurable, repeatable growth loops.*
- **Key Differentiators:**
  - Full‑cycle growth loop automation
  - Transparent, auditable AI reasoning
  - Plug‑and‑play integration framework
