# AUREA Go-To-Market

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how AUREA acquires, activates, and retains customers.

A great product with no distribution is not a business.

The strategy targets the wedge defined in `[[personas]]`.

---

# 2. GTM Thesis

```text
AUREA sells better growth decisions
to founders who own growth
and cannot yet afford a growth team.

The fastest path is founder-to-founder trust,
product-led activation,
and visible, measurable results.
```

AUREA should not start with enterprise sales motions.

The wedge buys self-serve, on the strength of a fast first result.

---

# 3. Motion — Product-Led with Founder Distribution

```text
Content + community (demand)
        ↓
Self-serve trial (product-led)
        ↓
Fast activation / first value (product)
        ↓
Paid conversion (product)
        ↓
Expansion (usage + tiers)
        ↓
Referral (founder networks)
```

Sales-assist is layered on only for Scale-tier accounts.

---

# 4. The Cold-Start Problem

AUREA's moat is accumulated learning, but a new customer arrives with none.

If AUREA is only useful *after* months of data, customers churn before the moat forms.

Day-1 value must exist before any per-account learning accumulates.

```text
Sources of day-one value:

Segment priors
  Cross-account patterns for the wedge (privacy-safe). See [[moat]].

Seeded playbooks
  Proven strategy templates for early-stage B2B SaaS.

Structured intelligence
  Research + strategy + experiment design are valuable
  even before any outcome is measured.

Fast first experiment
  Get to a measured outcome within the first week.
```

The onboarding must be engineered to reach activation fast (see `[[metrics]]` §5).

---

# 5. Onboarding Flow

```text
Sign up
  ↓
Connect the business (guided; assisted by research)
  ↓
Confirm ICP (proposed by AUREA, edited by user)
  ↓
Set one goal
  ↓
Receive a seeded strategy + first experiment proposal
  ↓
Launch the first experiment
  ↓
See the first measured outcome
  ↓
Record the first learning  ← activation
```

Every step should reduce effort by proposing, not asking blank questions.

Context over prompts.

---

# 6. Demand Generation

Channels tuned to the wedge (Brazilian early-stage B2B SaaS founders):

```text
Founder communities
  Startup Slack/Discord/WhatsApp groups, accelerator cohorts.

Content
  Real teardowns: "how a SaaS like yours found its first channel",
  benchmark reports powered by AUREA's own data (see [[moat]]).

Ecosystem
  Accelerators, VC platform teams, no-code / SaaS tool communities.

The product as marketing
  Public benchmark data and case studies AUREA uniquely can produce.

Founder-led
  The AUREA team publicly using AUREA to grow AUREA.
```

AUREA should dogfood: grow itself using its own growth loop, and publish it.

---

# 7. Benchmark Reports as a Wedge Weapon

Once AUREA has outcome data across the segment, it can publish benchmarks no competitor has.

```text
"Median CAC for seed-stage Brazilian B2B SaaS: R$ X"
"The channel that works first for SaaS at < R$100k MRR"
```

Benchmarks:

```text
Attract the exact ICP.
Prove the moat exists.
Create inbound demand.
Reinforce that AUREA knows this segment.
```

This turns the moat into a distribution engine.

---

# 8. Conversion

```text
Trial gives real value (a measured outcome), not a demo.

Paywall sits at the point of continued value:
  more experiments, more channels, execution, benchmarks.

Pricing anchored to the cost of a growth function, not a tool.
  See [[business-model]].
```

---

# 9. Retention and Expansion

```text
Weekly growth ritual inside AUREA
  A recurring reason to return: the next decision.

Compounding value
  Each cycle makes AUREA's recommendations better for that account.

Expansion
  More usage, higher autonomy tiers, more workspaces/seats.
```

Retention is a product problem first, a marketing problem second.

If the loop delivers, retention follows.

---

# 10. Referral

The wedge is a dense network.

```text
Founders talk to founders.
A founder who grew with AUREA is the best acquisition channel.
Build referral into the product and into case studies.
```

---

# 11. What to Avoid Early

```text
Enterprise sales motions.
Long custom implementations.
Serving segments outside the wedge because a lead appeared.
Competing on integration count instead of decision quality.
```

Focus is the strategy.

---

# 12. GTM Principle

```text
Win one segment completely
by delivering a fast first result,
compounding value per account,
and turning accumulated learning into
both a moat and a distribution engine.
```

Distribution and product are the same effort: the loop that grows the customer is the loop that grows AUREA.
