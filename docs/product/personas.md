# AUREA Personas and Wedge

## 1. Purpose

This document defines who AUREA is for.

AUREA's product documentation describes how AUREA helps a customer define *their* ICP.

This document defines AUREA's *own* ICP.

A platform that serves everyone serves no one.

The following personas and wedge are the initial commercial focus.

They are assumptions to be validated, not permanent constraints.

---

# 2. The Wedge

AUREA should not launch as a horizontal "growth OS for any business".

Horizontal breadth is death by dilution.

The initial wedge is:

```text
Brazilian B2B SaaS companies
Seed to Series A
10 to 50 employees
Monthly recurring revenue R$50k to R$1M
A founder or head of growth who owns the number
No dedicated growth/data team
Already spending on marketing but unsure what works
```

### Why this wedge

```text
High pain
  →  growth is existential at this stage

Underserved
  →  too small for an agency, too early for a growth team

Measurable
  →  SaaS metrics are clean (MRR, CAC, churn, conversion)

Fast loop
  →  digital acquisition produces outcomes in days, not quarters

Reachable
  →  concentrated in known communities and channels

Willingness to pay
  →  a validated experiment that lifts conversion pays for AUREA many times over
```

---

# 3. Why Not Horizontal

A local restaurant, a global enterprise, and a SaaS startup have nothing in common in:

```text
Metrics
Channels
Sales cycle
Data availability
Buying process
Budget
```

Serving all of them means AUREA's intelligence cannot specialize.

Specialization is the moat.

See `[[moat]]`.

AUREA expands to adjacent segments only after dominating the wedge.

# 4. Expansion Path

```text
Wedge
  Brazilian B2B SaaS (seed–Series A)
        ↓
Adjacent 1
  LATAM B2B SaaS
        ↓
Adjacent 2
  B2B SaaS globally
        ↓
Adjacent 3
  Other high-measurability digital B2B (agencies, marketplaces, infoproducts)
        ↓
Horizontal
  Broader SMB growth
```

Each expansion should reuse accumulated learning, not restart it.

# 5. Primary Persona — The Growth-Owning Founder

```text
Name role:        Founder / CEO of an early-stage B2B SaaS
Owns:             The growth number, personally
Team:             No growth team; maybe one marketer or agency
Tools today:      GA4, a CRM, a spreadsheet, an ad account, ChatGPT
Time:             Almost none
Skill gap:        Knows the product, not paid acquisition or experimentation
```

### Jobs to be done

```text
Tell me what to do next to grow.
Tell me if what I'm doing is working.
Stop me from wasting money.
Give me the confidence a growth hire would give, without the hire.
```

### What they fear

```text
Spending on the wrong channel.
Being fooled by vanity metrics.
Falling behind competitors.
Hiring a R$20k/month growth lead too early.
```

### Why they buy AUREA

```text
AUREA is the growth brain they cannot yet afford to hire,
that also remembers everything and improves over time.
```

# 6. Secondary Persona — The Solo Head of Growth

```text
Role:      First growth hire at a Series A SaaS
Owns:      Pipeline and CAC
Team:      Themselves, plus freelancers
Pain:      Drowning in execution, no time for strategy or analysis
```

### Why they buy AUREA

```text
AUREA is leverage.

It does the research, strategy structuring, experiment design,
and measurement analysis that would otherwise require
an analyst and a strategist they do not have.
```

# 7. Anti-Personas

AUREA should explicitly *not* target these initially.

```text
Enterprises
  Long sales cycles, procurement, custom needs.

Agencies
  Different economics; potential channel partner later, not first customer.

Pre-revenue startups
  No baseline, no budget, no data to learn from.

Local/offline SMBs
  Low measurability, weak digital loop.

Large ecommerce
  Different metric surface; a later adjacent, not the wedge.
```

Saying no to these is a strategic act.

# 8. Buying Context

```text
Trigger:        A growth plateau, a fundraise, or a failed hire/agency.
Evaluation:     Self-serve trial; the founder tests it personally.
Decision:       The founder decides; no committee.
Time to value:  Must be days, not months (see cold-start in [[gtm]]).
Objection:      "Is this just another ChatGPT wrapper?"
Answer:         The Growth Brain — persistent context, measured outcomes,
                compounding learning. See [[moat]].
```

---

# 9. Persona Principle

AUREA's early product, messaging, integrations, and defaults should be tuned for one person:

```text
The early-stage B2B SaaS founder
who owns growth,
has no growth team,
and needs better decisions now.
```

Every roadmap decision should be tested against:

```text
Does this make that person's next growth decision better?
```

If not, it can wait.
