# AUREA Product Roadmap

## 1. Roadmap Philosophy

AUREA should not attempt to build a complete autonomous growth platform from the beginning.

The product should progressively increase its intelligence and autonomy.

The roadmap follows:

```text
Understand
→ Recommend
→ Execute
→ Learn
→ Automate
→ Autonomously Optimize
```

---

## MVP Priority

The first valuable version of AUREA should prove:

```text
Business Context
      ↓
Goal
      ↓
Research
      ↓
Strategy
      ↓
Experiment
      ↓
Recommendation
```

The MVP does not need full autonomous execution.

The first objective is to prove that AUREA can provide genuinely useful growth intelligence.

Only after that should execution and autonomy become primary product priorities.
